# Final Review Platform Context

This context defines the product language for the V2 final-review platform. It keeps user-facing product concepts distinct from implementation details.

## Language

**课程资料库**:
The student-facing product area where learners find course-bound PDF review materials with stable supply and lightweight value-added services.
_Avoid_: 学习资料库, 泛资料库, 资料广场

**服务院系**:
The currently supported learner scope: software college students from the project owner's school, with no general multi-school discovery promise.
_Avoid_: 多院校平台, 全国高校资料库, 全校通用平台

**课程**:
The primary catalog unit in the 课程资料库. Learners enter review materials through courses rather than through school, college, or major discovery.
_Avoid_: 院校入口, 专业入口, 泛分类入口

**资料稳定供应**:
The product promise that course review PDFs remain available, organized, and dependable for software college learners.
_Avoid_: 临时网盘, 一次性分享, 松散资料群

**资料保障**:
The student-facing wording for the course-material service promise, covering stable PDF availability, organized updates, download access, and unobtrusive watermarking.
_Avoid_: 附加服务, 会员权益, AI 服务包

**资料保障承诺**:
The limited first-version promise behind 资料保障: materials are organized by course, PDFs remain steadily downloadable, updates are maintained, and downloads include light watermarks.
_Avoid_: 必考命中, 老师原版, 答疑服务, AI 精准总结, 全覆盖承诺

**轻水印**:
A subtle ownership and traceability layer on course review materials that should not interfere with reading.
_Avoid_: 重水印, 影响阅读的防盗标记

**资料运营工作台**:
The admin-facing product area where operators maintain courses, upload and archive PDF materials, and monitor material availability.
_Avoid_: 营销后台, 学生端资料库, 内容展示页

**设计语言**:
The shared product feel for both student and admin surfaces: clean, trustworthy, lightly academic, and centered on course materials.
_Avoid_: 活动页风格, 强营销风格, 纯后台风格, 过度 AI 感

**课程社区**:
The future social layer around courses and course materials, covering discussion, contribution, review, and experience sharing.
_Avoid_: 泛社交, 朋友圈, 独立信息流

**资料库原生体验**:
The primary student experience centered on finding, trusting, and downloading course PDF materials, with AI treated as contextual assistance around courses and materials rather than the main entry point.
_Avoid_: 全屏营销主页, AI 聊天首页, 强 AI 原生入口
